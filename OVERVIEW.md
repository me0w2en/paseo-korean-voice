# Korean Voice

Korean dictation and voice-mode speech for Paseo.

- Replaces the fixed English Whisper prompt with a Korean prompt and your glossary
- Removes Whisper hallucinations such as "시청해 주셔서 감사합니다"
- Speaks voice-mode replies with macOS Korean voices (free, offline)

Runs a local OpenAI-compatible gateway on `127.0.0.1:6791`; point Paseo's OpenAI speech settings at it. Settings: **Settings → Plugins → Korean Voice**. macOS only.

한국어 받아쓰기·음성 모드를 위한 로컬 음성 게이트웨이입니다.
